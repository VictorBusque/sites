What makes a good explanation

Make complicated things feel obvious.

A good technical explanation doesn't just tell you what happens. It gives you a mental model you can carry around afterwards.

  1. 01
    Start with a question. Every investigation begins with something worth being curious about.
  2. 02
    Follow what moves. Packets, bytes, replicas, signals and requests are usually where the story lives.
  3. 03
    Show the scale. Sometimes the fastest way to understand a system is to make its size impossible to ignore.
