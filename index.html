What the collection is for

A quieter way to hold a useful idea.

These are not summaries with illustrations added after the fact. The visual is the route through the explanation: a packet changes hops, a signal becomes a distance, a network reveals its local edge.

  • One concrete subjectStart with something you can point to: a phone, a seat screen, a satellite, a grid.
  • One legible routeKeep track of what moves, what changes, and which boundary matters.
  • One durable takeawayThe result should still make sense when all the motion stops.
