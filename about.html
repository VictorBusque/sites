01Who I am

I'm Víctor Busqué, a software engineer in Barcelona. For the last eight years I've worked on NLP and generative AI in production — LLM gateways, agent runtimes, platforms that hundreds of engineers open every morning. That's the job. This page is about the person doing it, and the strange way his head works.

I'm an avid visual learner. I love reading, and I do plenty of it — but an idea stays foreign until I can see it move. A simulation, a figure running the real process, an animation that shows the thing clearly: that's when it clicks. Everything I've built, and this whole site, comes out of that one trait.
